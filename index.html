📖 How to Use the Soroban (Japanese Abacus Guide)
▼

The Japanese Soroban (そろばん) is a base-10 counting frame perfected in the 1930s with a 1:4 bead ratio.

1. Active vs Inactive Beads only have a numerical value when they are touching the beam (the center horizontal bar). Beads resting against the outer top or bottom frame have zero value.
2. Heaven Bead (Upper) The single upper bead is worth 5 when pushed down against the beam. When pushed up away from the beam, it is inactive (0).
3. Earth Beads (Lower) The 4 lower beads are worth 1 each when pushed up against the beam. Clicking any lower bead pushes all beads above or below it to prevent gaps, mirroring physical wood pushing.
4. Unit Dots (Hoshi 星) The inlaid white dots on the beam mark unit intervals (usually every 3 rods, like thousands separators). Click any dot to choose your active unit rod!

Keyboard Navigation: Use Tab or Arrow Keys to focus beads. Press Space or Enter to move beads. Use C to reset and R for random numbers.
